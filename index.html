02 — About

Built for
the drop

NVRMND started in a bedroom studio outside Trondheim with a cracked kick sample and too much time. Six years later the same kick — heavier, uglier, tuned to the floor — is the signature.

The sound sits where raw hardstyle meets something colder and more industrial: distorted low end, screeching leads, and long builds that hold the room a beat longer than comfortable before letting go.
