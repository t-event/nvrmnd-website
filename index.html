02 / About

Built for
the drop

NVRMND is hardstyle out of Mo i Rana, a town on the Norwegian coast. Years of tracks that never left the apartment, built on a pair of monitors one late night at a time, until Need Me became the first one to get out.

The name is an attitude more than a word. The sound sits where raw hardstyle meets something colder and more industrial: distorted low end, screeching leads, and long builds that hold the room a beat longer than comfortable before letting go. Raised on Project One and Vertile, aimed at the front row.

NVRMND has played plenty of rooms as a DJ, and has never played his own music to one of them yet. That changes. The next tracks are already in the works.
