02 / About

Built for
the drop

NVRMND is hardstyle out of Mo i Rana, a town on the Norwegian coast. No studio complex, no session players. Every kick, lead and scream gets built in an apartment, on a pair of monitors, one late night at a time.

The sound sits where raw hardstyle meets something colder and more industrial: distorted low end, screeching leads, and long builds that hold the room a beat longer than comfortable before letting go. Need Me is the first one out. It won't be the last.
