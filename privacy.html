Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: this site sets no cookies of its own, has no analytics, and collects nothing about you. The music players from Spotify and SoundCloud do set cookies, so they stay switched off until you allow them. Below is the longer version.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no contact form, no newsletter signup, no account, no shopping basket and no tracking. We do not know who visits this site, and we have no way of finding out.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking. The only cookies that can appear come from the Spotify and SoundCloud players, and only if you allow them. See Embedded players below.

We do store one thing in your browser: your answer to the player question, kept in local storage under the name nvrmnd:embeds. It holds a single word, either allowed or refused. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service from GitHub, Inc. Like every web server, GitHub records requests it receives, which can include your IP address and browser. That data belongs to GitHub, not to us, and we never see it. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and details about your browser. Google states that it does not use this to build advertising profiles, and does not set cookies for font requests. Even so, this is a transfer of data to Google that happens without you being asked, so we would rather you know than not.

See Google Fonts and privacy.

Embedded players

There is a Spotify player and a SoundCloud player in the music section. Both are loaded from those companies, and both set their own cookies.

Because of that, they are not loaded until you allow them. On your first visit you are asked. Until you answer, nothing is requested from Spotify or SoundCloud and neither company knows you are here.

  • If you allow them, the players load straight away, and on every later visit they load immediately without asking again.
  • If you refuse, the players stay switched off and you get a plain link to each platform instead.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie choice link at the bottom of the front page.

Once a player is loaded, that company's own terms apply. Both are likely to set cookies and to register that you played something.

  • Spotify privacy policy
  • SoundCloud privacy policy

Links to other sites

The site links out to Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, SoundCloud, YouTube, Amazon Music and Instagram. Once you follow a link, you are on their site and under their privacy policy. We have no control over what they do.

Photographs

Photographs on this site are taken at public events. If you appear in one and would rather not, write to us and it will be removed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. Since we hold none, there is nothing to request. If you believe otherwise, get in touch and we will look into it.
